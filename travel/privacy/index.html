Travel – Privacy Policy

App: Travel (MyTravelApp) for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Android
Last updated: 5 November 2026

In short: Travel keeps your trips on your devices and, if you turn on Cloud Sync, in your own account so they are the same on all your devices. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and your information is never sold or shared for marketing. You can delete your account and its cloud data at any time from inside the app.

Who is responsible

Travel is made by Kevin O'Neill, an individual developer in Ireland, who is the data controller for the information described here. Contact: kevinaoneill1@gmail.com.

Information on your device

Everything you enter in Travel – trips, itinerary items, travellers, costs, checklists, notes, destinations, service providers, documents and photos – is stored on your device. Without Cloud Sync, it stays there and is not sent to me or anyone else (apart from the look-ups described under Online services below).

Cloud Sync (optional)

If you create an account and turn on Cloud Sync, the following is stored in your account so that your devices stay in step:

  • Account: your email address and a password (the password is handled by Firebase Authentication and is never visible to me).
  • Travel data: your trips and everything in them, including the names of travellers you add, costs, notes and checklists, and your destinations, service providers, activities, checklist templates and app notes.
  • Documents: files you attach to trips (for example booking confirmations, tickets, boarding passes and photos), with their file name, size and type.

This information is used only to provide Cloud Sync to you. Only your account can read it; access is enforced by security rules on the server. I can technically access the project's database as its administrator, but I do not look at your data except when you ask me to help with a problem.

Cloud Sync is provided by Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Cloud Storage for Firebase), operated by Google, which processes the data on my behalf. Google may process data in data centres outside the European Economic Area, protected by Google's data processing terms and the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. See Firebase privacy and security.

Online services the app uses

To show weather, maps, place names, exchange rates and destination information, Travel asks these services for information. They receive what is needed for the request, plus your device's IP address as with any internet request. None of them receives your account details or your trip details beyond what is listed.

Location

If you allow it, Travel uses your device's location only while you use the app, to show the local weather and your position on the Travel Map. Your location is not stored in your account and is not used for tracking. You can turn location access off at any time in your device settings.

Photos, files and sharing into Travel

Travel only accesses the photos and files you choose to attach, or that you share to Travel from another app.

What Travel does not do

  • No advertising, no advertising identifiers and no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • Your information is never sold, rented or shared for marketing.

Why the information is used (legal basis)

Information is processed to provide the features you ask for – storing your trips and, if you choose, syncing them through your account (performance of a contract, GDPR Article 6(1)(b)). Location is used only with your permission.

How long it is kept

  • Data on your device is kept until you delete it or delete the app.
  • Cloud Sync data is kept while your account exists. When you delete a trip or document, it is removed from your account; deleted data can remain in the service's short-term recovery copies for up to 7 days before it is gone for good.
  • When you delete your account, its cloud data is deleted as described below.

Deleting your account

In Travel, go to Settings › Cloud Sync › Delete Account. After you confirm with your password, your account and everything it keeps in the cloud – trips, lists, settings and documents including the files – are deleted. The copy on the device you are using is kept until you remove it, so you can still make a backup. You can also ask for deletion by email at kevinaoneill1@gmail.com.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal data, and to object to or restrict its processing. Most of this you can do directly in the app (edit or delete anything; export a full backup in Tools › Backup & Restore; delete your account). For anything else, email kevinaoneill1@gmail.com. You can also complain to the Data Protection Commission in Ireland, or to the data protection authority where you live.

Children

Travel is not directed at children, and an account should be created by an adult.

Security

Information sent to and from the cloud is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and stored by Google with encryption at rest. Access to each account's data is limited to that account by server security rules.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
