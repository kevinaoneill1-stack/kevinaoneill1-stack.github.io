Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

In short: Best Gem Match does not collect, store, or share any personal information. There are no ads, no analytics, and no accounts.

This privacy policy explains how the Best Gem Match app for iPhone ("the app") handles your information. The app is developed by Kevin O'Neill ("I", "me").

Information I collect

None. The app does not collect any personal information or usage data. In particular, the app does not:

  • ask you to create an account or sign in
  • collect your name, email address, location, contacts, or photos
  • use analytics or tracking tools
  • show advertising or use advertising identifiers
  • send any data about you or your device to me or to third parties

Data stored on your device

The app may save information such as your game progress, scores, and settings locally on your device so you can pick up where you left off. This data stays on your device and is never sent to me. Deleting the app removes it.

Third-party services

The app does not include any third-party services that collect data. If you download the app from the App Store, Apple handles that transaction under its own privacy policy.

Children's privacy

Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If the app's handling of data changes, for example if features such as ads or analytics are added in a future version, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top of this page will change.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact me at you@example.com.
